About us · เกี่ยวกับเรา

Small shop, big flavour.

Crepe 90 is a cosy, family-run crepe shop tucked into Ban Noan Road Soi 1. Our batter is spread paper-thin and cooked until golden and crisp, then folded around generous fillings, Nutella right to the edges.

Grab one on the go, or take a seat inside with a cold drink. Our matcha coconut is made with fresh coconut water.

  • Sweet & savoury crepes from ฿30
  • Matcha coconut, peach tea & smoothies
  • Seats inside · takeaway ready in minutes
